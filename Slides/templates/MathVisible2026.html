Space of multivariate splines

If \(\Delta \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n\) is a polyhedral complex and \(r \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}\), then
\[ \mathcal{S}^r(\Delta) = \left\{ f \in C^r(\Delta)\; :\; f|_\sigma \in \mathbb{R}[x_1,\dots,x_n] \text{ for all } \sigma \in \Delta_n \right\}, \text{and} \] \[\mathcal{S}_d^r(\Delta)= \bigl\{ f\in \mathcal{S}^r(\Delta)\colon \deg(f|_\sigma)\leq d\; \text{for all} \; \sigma\in\Delta_n\bigr\}.\]

The set $\mathcal{S}_d^r(\Delta)$ is a real vector space.



Embed $\Delta$ in $\{x_{n+1}=1\}\subseteq\mathbb R^{n+1}$, \[\Rightarrow\mathcal S^r(\hat{\Delta})=\bigoplus\limits_{d\geq 0}\mathcal S^r(\hat{\Delta})_d\] is a graded module, and \[\dim \mathcal S_d^r(\Delta)=\dim \mathcal S^r(\hat{\Delta})_d.\]

Simplicial complex

A simplicial complex gives rise to a chain complex: the \(R\)-module \(C_i\) is generated by the oriented \(i\)-simplices $ [v_{j_0},\dots,v_{j_i}] = (-1)^{\mathrm{sgn}(\rho)} [v_{j_{\rho(0)}},\dots,v_{j_{\rho(i)}}] $, and \[ \partial([\sigma])= \sum_{j=0}^n (-1)^j [v_{i_0},\dots,\hat{v}_{i_j},\dots,v_{i_n}] \quad \text{(\(\partial\partial=0\))} \]

For example: \[ [\sigma] = [v_1,v_2,v_3]\quad [\tau] = [v_2,v_3]\quad [\tau'] = [v_1,v_2] \] $ \partial([v_1,v_2,v_3])= [v_2,v_3]-[v_1,v_3]+[v_1,v_2] $

We extend \(\partial\) by linearity

\[ \mathcal{C}:\; 0 \to C_2 \xrightarrow{\partial_2} C_{1} \xrightarrow{\partial_1} C_0 \to 0. \]

Define the complex of ideals...

\[ \mathcal{J}(\sigma)=\langle 0 \rangle \]
\[ \mathcal{J}(\tau)=\langle \ell_\tau^{r+1} \rangle \]
\[ \mathcal{J}(\gamma) = \sum \langle \ell_\tau^{r+1}\colon \tau \ni \gamma\rangle \]
\[ \mathcal{R}/\mathcal{J}:\; 0 \to \bigoplus_{\sigma \in \Delta_2}\mathcal{R}(\sigma) \xrightarrow{\partial_2} \bigoplus_{\tau \in \Delta_{1}^{0}}\mathcal{R/J}(\tau) \xrightarrow{\partial_1} \bigoplus_{\gamma \in \Delta_0^{0}}\mathcal{R/J}(\gamma) \to 0 \]
\[ \dim \mathcal{S}^r_d(\Delta)=\dim H_n(\mathcal{R}/\mathcal{J})_d =\ker(\partial_2). \]

Thus,


$\dim \mathcal S^r_d(\Delta)=\hspace{-0.1cm}\sum\limits_{\sigma\in\Delta_2}\hspace{-0.1cm}\dim \mathcal{R}(\sigma)_d-\hspace{-0.1cm}\sum\limits_{\tau\in\Delta_{1}^0}\hspace{-0.1cm}\dim \mathcal{R}/\mathcal{J}(\tau)_d +\hspace{-0.1cm}\sum\limits_{\gamma\in\Delta_{0}^0}\hspace{-0.1cm}\dim \mathcal{R}/\mathcal{J}(\gamma)_d +\dim H_{0}(\mathcal J)_d$

Dimension of spline spaces

 Theorem:  For a simplicial complex $\Delta\subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ \begin{align*} & {\scriptsize \dim S_d^r(\Delta) \leq \binom{d+2}{2}+f_1^0\binom{d+2-(r+1)}{2}-\sum_{i,\,\tilde{t}_i=1}^{f_0^0}\binom{d+2-(r+1)}{2}}\\[.5ex] &{\scriptsize -\sum_{i=1,\, \tilde{t}_i\geq 2}^{f_0^0}\biggl[\tilde{t}_i\binom{d+2-(r+1)}{2}\hspace{-0.1cm}-\tilde{b}_i\,\binom{d+2-\tilde{\Omega}_i}{2}-\tilde{a}_i\,\binom{d+2-(\tilde{\Omega}_i+1)}{2}\biggr]} \end{align*}   $\tilde{t}_i=$ number of edges connecting the vertex $\gamma_i$ to the boundary or vertices of lower index,
     ${\small \tilde\Omega_i=\bigr\lfloor\frac{\tilde{t}_i r}{\tilde{t}_i-1}\bigr\rfloor+1,\quad \tilde{a}_i=\tilde{t}_i (r+1) +(1-\tilde{t}_i)\tilde \Omega_i,}$  and   ${\small \tilde{b}_i=\tilde{t}_i-1-\tilde{a}_i.}$

Koszul homology

  •  Let \( \Bbbk \) be a field, \( R=\Bbbk[x_1,\dots,x_n] \), and \( \bm{y}=\{y_1,\dots,y_m\}\subset R \). Let \( I=\langle \bm{y} \rangle \).

  •  Let \( F \) be a free module with basis \( e_1,\dots,e_m \), and define \[ \varphi:F \to I, \qquad \varphi(e_i)=y_i. \]
  •  The Koszul complex:
\[ \mathcal{K}_i(\bm{y})=\bigwedge^i F, \quad \partial_i(z_1\wedge\cdots\wedge z_i) = \sum_{j=1}^i (-1)^{j+1} \varphi(z_j)\, z_1\wedge\cdots\widehat{z_j}\cdots\wedge z_i \]

  •  \(\mathsf{H}_0(\bm{y}) = R/I\) and \(\mathsf{H}_1(\bm{y}) = \frac{\ker(\partial_1)}{\operatorname{Im}(\partial_2)}\) (first Koszul homology), where

$ \ker(\partial_1)=\left\{ \sum \alpha_i e_i \;:\; \sum \alpha_i y_i = 0\right\} $
(syzygies)
$$ \partial_2(z\wedge z')= \varphi(z)z' - \varphi(z')z $$
(Koszul syzygies)
 •  If \( \bm{y} \) is minimal: $ \mathsf{H}_\bullet(I) \cong \mathsf{H}_\bullet(\bm{y}). $

Koszul homology and splines

Consider splines with constant smoothness $\bm r$ along hyperplanes.
Let \( \mathcal{A} = \bigcup_{i=1}^n H_i \subset \mathbb{R}^3 \) be a central hyperplane arrangement, and \[ \ell^{\bm{r}} = \{\ell_H^{r(H)+1} : H\in\mathcal{A}\}, \qquad I=\langle \ell^{\mathbf{r}} \rangle. \]
Theorem (Checa et al., 2026):   •   \( H_1(\mathcal{J}) \) is a quotient of \( \mathsf{H}_1(\ell^{\mathbf{r}}) \), it has finite length
  •   If \( \mathcal{A} \) is generic: $ H_1(\mathcal{J}) \cong \mathsf{H}_1(\ell^{\mathbf{r}}) $
Dimension bound:
$ \dim \mathcal{S}^{\bm{r}}_d(\Sigma^\mathcal{A}) \le 2\dim R_d + \sum_{\tau} \dim \mathcal J(\tau)_d - \sum_{\gamma} \dim \mathcal J(\gamma)_d + \dim \mathsf{H}_1(\ell^{\mathbf{r}})_d - \dim \mathsf{H}_0(\ell^{\mathbf{r}})_d $

For \( d \gg 0 \): $\quad \dim \mathcal{S}^{\bm{r}}_d(\Sigma^\mathcal{A}) = 2\dim R_d + \sum_{\tau} \dim \mathcal J(\tau)_d - \sum_{\gamma} \dim \mathcal J(\gamma)_d $

Generic case:
$\qquad \dim \mathcal{S}^{\bm{r}}_d(\Sigma^\mathcal{A}) = 2\binom{d+2}{2} + 2\sum_{H_1\neq H_2} \binom{d-r(H_1)-r(H_2)}{2} + \dim \mathsf H_1(\ell^\bm{r})_d - \dim \mathsf{H}_0(\ell^{\bm{r}})_d $

Superpline spaces

The super-spline space $\mathcal S^{r,\bm s}_{d }(\Delta)$ is the set of $C^r$-splines on $\Delta$ with supersmoothness $s_\beta$ at $\beta\in\Delta_{i}$:
\begin{equation*} \mathcal S^{r, \bm s}_{d }(\Delta) = \bigl\{f\in \mathcal S^{r}_{d }(\Delta) \colon f\in C^{s_\beta}(\beta) \; \text{for all}\; \beta \in \Delta_{i}\bigr\} \end{equation*}
where $f|_\sigma$ have common derivatives up to order $s$ at $\beta$ for all $\sigma\supseteq\beta$ . In $\R^2$, if $[\gamma,\gamma']=\tau\in\Delta_{1}^\circ$, take \[ \mathcal J(\tau)=\langle \ell_\tau^{r+1}\rangle\cap \mathfrak m_\gamma^{s+1}\cap \mathfrak m_{\gamma'}^{s+1}\] where ${\mathfrak m}_{\gamma}={\mathfrak m}_{\gamma'}$ are the maximal ideals at ${\gamma}$ and ${\gamma'}$.
