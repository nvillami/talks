Spline functions (definition)

Spline functions are piecewise polynomial functions with a specified degree of smoothness on polyhedral partitions in \( \mathbb{R}^n \).

Ex: A spline function on \([a,c] \cup [c,b]\) is any function of the form:

\[ f(x) = \begin{cases} f_1(x) & \text{if } x \in [a, c] \\ f_2(x) & \text{if } x \in [c, b]; \quad \text{for } f_1(x), f_2(x) \in \mathbb{R}[x]. \end{cases} \]

Taking \( a = 0 \), \( c = 2 \), and \( b = 4 \), then:

\[ f(x) = \begin{cases} x & \text{if } x \in [0,2] \\ -x^2 + 5x - 4 & \text{if } x \in [2,4] \end{cases} \]
